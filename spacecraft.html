Suborbital Spaceplane

Orbit Horizon

Designed for smooth runway takeoffs and landings, Horizon carries explorers past the Kármán line for four minutes of pure weightlessness.

  • Capacity: 6 Passengers + 2 Pilots
  • Max Altitude: 105 km (344,000 ft)
  • Windows: 12 Oversized Circular Viewports
Deep Space & Orbital Capsule

Orbit Odyssey

Equipped with a transparent nose-cone cupola, Odyssey docks with Low Earth Orbit stations and performs 7-day lunar flyby missions.

  • Capacity: 4 Civilian Astronauts
  • Range: Low Earth Orbit & Lunar Orbit
  • Feature: 360° Panoramic Observation Dome

Safety & Engineering

Launch Escape System

Integrated solid-fuel abort motors capable of separating the crew cabin safely within milliseconds at any altitude.

Triple-Redundant Avionics

Three independent flight computers continuously cross-check navigation, cabin pressure, and trajectory data.

Custom G-Force Seating

3D-scanned ergonomic seats tailored to each passenger's body to minimize physical strain during ascent and re-entry.
