Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your journey beyond Earth.

Is space travel safe?

Safety is our priority. Every journey includes professional training, essential safety equipment, and an experienced crew.

Do I need previous space travel experience?

No previous experience is required. All passengers receive training before departure.

How long does each journey last?

The Explorer package lasts 3 hours, Voyager lasts 5 days, and Pioneer is an extended mission.

What is included in the packages?

Every package includes safety training, space travel equipment, and professional crew support. Additional features depend on your selected package.

How can I book a journey?

Visit our Book Now page, select your preferred package, and complete the reservation form.

Can I cancel my reservation?

You can contact our team to request a cancellation. Cancellation conditions depend on the selected package.

Contact our team for more information about your journey.
